# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors to a personal blog and portfolio. Readers looking for writing about code, creativity, and building meaningful things. Fellow developers, queer creators, and internet wanderers who appreciate craft and warmth. They arrive from links, RSS, or search; they stay because the space feels intentional and alive.

## Product Purpose

AutumnsGrove is Autumn's corner of the internet: a place for words, projects, and quiet thoughts. It exists to share writing with personality, demonstrate projects with context, and create a digital space that feels like home. Success looks like: someone reads a post, notices the vine annotations in the margin, feels the warmth of the palette, and bookmarks the site because it felt like a real place made by a real person.

## Brand Personality

**Warm, introspective, bold.**

The voice is first-person, honest, and unhurried. Write like you're helping someone speak, not perform. The site should feel like a midnight tea shop: warm lighting, good company, unhurried conversation. But also confident; this is someone who builds things unapologetically and shares them with conviction. Queer identity is woven in naturally, not performatively.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS / startup sites.** No blue gradients, hero metric cards, identical feature grids, stock photography, "Get Started Free" CTAs.
- **Minimal dev blogs.** No bare markdown dumps with zero personality, monochrome color schemes, Times New Roman fallbacks, "powered by Jekyll" energy.
- **Lattice tenant sites.** This is NOT a Grove tenant. It should not look like grove.place or any other site using the Lattice framework. Its own identity, its own colors, its own components.

## Design Principles

1. **Handmade over templated.** Every element should feel intentionally placed, not generated from a component library. Visible craft signals care.
2. **Warmth as structure.** Color, typography, and spacing should create the feeling of a welcoming space, not just convey information. The aesthetic does emotional work.
3. **Content breathes.** Give writing room. Vine annotations float alongside, not cramped. Whitespace is generous but varied (not uniform padding everywhere).
4. **Personality in the details.** The site's character shows in small touches: how tags look, how the theme toggles, how error pages feel. Not through gimmicks, but through considered choices.
5. **Show, don't decorate.** Every visual choice serves the content or the mood. No decoration for its own sake, but no austerity either. The line between "enough" and "too much" is drawn by whether it helps someone feel at home.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum contrast ratios
- Reduced motion support (prefers-reduced-motion already implemented)
- Skip-to-content link present
- Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements
- No reliance on color alone for meaning
